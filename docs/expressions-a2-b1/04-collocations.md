# Collocations — A2 to B1 (American English)
### 346 items · organized by the logic that fixes Thai errors

**Why this organization.** Collocations are unbounded — Shin & Nation found 2,052 of them under just the first 100 pivot words. You cannot teach "all of them," so this list uses the **delexical verb spine**: twelve verbs that carry most everyday collocation *and* that Thai learners systematically confuse, because Thai maps `make / do / take / have` onto the single verb **ทำ**.

**Legend.** ⚠️ = high Thai-interference risk · 🇺🇸 = American-preferred form · `T1/T2/T3` = tier

## 10. PLAY / WATCH / LISTEN / READ — กิจกรรม (โซนอันตรายของนักเรียนไทย)
> **The biggest Thai trap in the whole book.** Thai uses **เล่น** for far more things than English uses *play*.

| ❌ Thai-influenced error | ✅ Correct American English | ไทย |
|---|---|---|

| *play Facebook* | **be on Facebook / check Facebook** | เล่นเฟซบุ๊ก |
| *play line* | **text on LINE / chat on LINE** | เล่นไลน์ |
| *play music* (ตัวเอง) | **play an instrument / play the guitar** | เล่นดนตรี |

| *play water* | **play in the water / go swimming** | เล่นน้ำ |
| *play ghost* | **watch a horror movie** | ดูหนังผี |

# PART 4 — Grammatical collocations (50 items) ⚠️ THE #1 THAI ERROR ZONE

> The research is explicit: **noun + preposition and adjective + preposition produce the highest error counts** for Thai learners, and Thai students routinely **omit an obligatory English preposition** because the Thai verb doesn't need one. Give this section its own colored pages.
