Introduction
The diagram illustrates the process of the formation of rain-shadow deserts from start to finish.
Overview
Overall, the process entails seven steps in total, starting with the approach of winds from the coast and culminating in dry winds reaching inland areas, resulting in a rain-shadow desert.
Body 1
To begin with, winds from the sea approach the coast before being pushed upwards. Subsequently, as the air rises, it becomes moist and cool. In the following step, the cool moist air forms clouds, which result in rainfall.
Body 2
With regard to the remaining stages of the process, after the rainfall, the dry air continues to move over the mountain before going down along the leeward side and reaching the bottom of the mountain, which continues towards inland areas. In the final step, when these dry winds reach the inland area, they lead to the formation of a rain-shadow desert, marking the completion of the rain-shadow desert formation process.
